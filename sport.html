What France watches

Football

Les Bleus won the World Cup in 1998 and 2018. Paris Saint-Germain, Olympique de Marseille and Olympique Lyonnais lead Ligue 1, and the Clásico-style rivalry between PSG and OM is fierce.

Rugby union

The XV de France play in the Six Nations. Toulouse, Toulon and La Rochelle are among Europe’s top clubs, and rugby is a passion in the south-west.

Cycling

The Tour de France runs for three weeks each July and finishes on the Champs-Élysées. Paris–Roubaix and the Critérium du Dauphiné are other classics.

Tennis

Roland-Garros in Paris is the clay-court Grand Slam, played each May and June.

Pétanque and motor racing

Pétanque is played on village squares across the south; the 24 Hours of Le Mans is the world’s most famous endurance race.
