French associations, schools and diaspora groups exist almost everywhere the French settled. Here is how to find yours.

Communities built to last

Alliance Française

A network of language and cultural centres in more than 130 countries, with classes, film nights, talks and exhibitions.

French schools abroad

Lycées français and bilingual schools following the French curriculum, a natural meeting point for families.

Chambers of commerce and networks

French chambers of commerce and professional networks for people working abroad.

Regional associations

Bretons, Alsatians, Corsicans and others have clubs abroad that keep local food, music and language alive.

Francophone communities

Quebec, Louisiana, Acadia, Belgium, Switzerland and West Africa: the wider French-speaking world shares the language.

Newcomers and supporters

Meet-ups for French people who have moved recently, and supporters’ clubs for football, rugby and the Tour de France.
