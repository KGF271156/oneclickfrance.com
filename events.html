Throughout the year

National dates worth knowing

Galette des Rois: January

An almond pastry shared at Epiphany, with a charm hidden inside that makes someone king or queen for the day.

Cannes Film Festival: May

The world’s best-known film festival, on the Riviera.

Fête de la Musique: 21 June

Free music in every street and square, born in France in 1982 and now celebrated in more than 100 countries.

Bastille Day: 14 July

The national day, with the military parade on the Champs-Élysées, fireworks and dancing in the street.

Beaujolais Nouveau: November

The new wine is released on the third Thursday of November, with parties around the world.
