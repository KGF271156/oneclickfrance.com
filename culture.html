What carries abroad

The French language

French is spoken on five continents and is an official language of more than 25 countries. The Académie française has guarded it since 1635, and Francophonie links the French-speaking world.

Art and architecture

From Gothic cathedrals and Versailles to the Impressionists, Rodin and the Pompidou Centre: France has some of the richest museums and monuments in the world.

Cinema and the chanson

The Lumière brothers invented cinema in Lyon in 1895; Cannes is the home of the world’s best-known film festival; and the chanson française, from Piaf to Brel and Gainsbourg, still carries the language.

Literature and ideas

Hugo, Flaubert, Proust, Camus, Beauvoir: French writers and thinkers have shaped world literature, and the Prix Goncourt is awarded each November.

Fashion and design

Paris is the home of haute couture, Chanel, Dior and Saint Laurent, and its Fashion Weeks set the tone for the year.

France abroad

Culture kept alive a generation on

Alliance Française branches in more than 130 countries, French lycées abroad and French-speaking communities from Quebec and Louisiana to Lebanon and the Indian Ocean keep French identity alive far from home.
